# Onboarding Copy Spec — `iam-claude`

Claude-original conversion strategy on the **I Am** UX framework (serif-on-cream
quiz rhythm, auto-advance single-selects, progress bar since 2026-09-12 (see
the last section), interstitial
breathers, name reuse, notification education before the OS dialog, streak
commitment moment, trial seeding, timeline paywall with delayed X, no visible
authentication). Written before `possibleonboardingcopy.md` was opened; not
based on it. Refined 2026-08-16 against the frame-by-frame I Am reference
(`docs/REFERENCE_IAM_SCREENS.md`) — its strongest beats (familiarity → primer →
habit helper → repetition → notifications; time devotion → streak goal → commit;
four belief questions → science → benefits; practice modes; "achieve" as the
last question) are reproduced here, re-voiced for Future Self.

**Emotional strategy — "the gap":** every screen frames a measurable distance
between today-you and future-you, and positions three small daily readings as
the honest mechanism that closes it. No fabricated statistics or testimonials
anywhere; the one science claim is worded inside what its cited sources
support (see _Sources_). Urgency comes from the user's own answers, mirrored
back. The word "mix" does not appear anywhere in this funnel.

Interaction key: `single` = auto-advance pills · `multi` = checkmarks +
Continue · `chips` = tag grid + Continue · `text` = free text · `info` =
interstitial + Continue · `config` = custom controls. Skip is top-right on
every question except motivation, the welcome, interstitials, notifications,
the streak commitment, theme, result and the paywall. `→ model` = write into
the normalized personalization model; `raw.*` keys land in `raw_answers`
(Supabase JSON, never analytics). Rhythm: bursts of 2–4 quick taps, then a
breather (interstitial, notification config, streak visual, science/benefits).

## Personalization honesty contract

Screen 31 claims only what the app does: `primary_goals`, `quote_interests`,
`affirmation_interests`, and `future_traits` weight the daily-set selector;
`life_goal` seeds the persistent widget and result copy; notification counts
and window write real preferences enforced server-side. Commitments the user
made in the funnel are echoed back verbatim and only when they were made:
`raw.daily_minutes` ("About {N} minute(s) a day."), `raw.streak_goal` ("First
goal: {N} days in a row." — the goal from screen 16, or 21 when it was skipped
and screen 17 committed to the default) and `raw.practice_modes` ("You'll
practice by …", "I'm not sure yet" is never echoed). Skipped steps add no row.
`raw.affirmation_familiarity` gates screen 7 (the primer is shown only to
"This is new for me"), which is what makes screen 6's "Your answer shapes how
we introduce them" true. Every other `raw.*` answer (habit helpers, vision,
the three beliefs, outcome goals) is stored in `raw_answers` and not yet used
by the app, so no screen claims that it is. If an answer is skipped, screen 31
copy degrades gracefully ("A balanced set of quotes to start — it sharpens as
you save favorites").

## Sources (science screen)

- Cohen, G. L. & Sherman, D. K. (2014). _The psychology of change:
  Self-affirmation and social psychological intervention._ Annual Review of
  Psychology, 65, 333–371 — review of self-affirmation effects on
  self-confidence, resilience under threat and well-being.
- Cascio, C. N. et al. (2016). _Self-affirmation activates brain systems
  associated with self-related processing and reward and is reinforced by
  future orientation._ Social Cognitive and Affective Neuroscience, 11(4),
  621–629 — fMRI evidence that self-affirmation engages reward and
  self-processing regions, most strongly when the affirmation is
  future-oriented.

The headline/sub wording on screen 23 was chosen to stay inside what these two
papers support; do not sharpen it (no percentages, no "proven").

## Authentication

None visible in the funnel (matches I Am exactly). The session is an anonymous
Supabase user from first launch; purchases attach to it. "Save your account"
(Apple / Google / email) lives in Settings → Account, offered contextually
after day-2 streak. Documented assumption: I Am ships no auth in onboarding, so
this variant defers identity to the main app.

Owner feedback on the live funnel; `src/features/onboarding/variants/iamClaude.ts`
stays the source of truth.

  "Nudges…"), "Quotes that match my goals" (was "Words…").

  `src/__tests__/variants.test.ts`.

  minutes", "3 readings a day…", and the same treatment in the other variants.
  Narrative numbers ("five years", "One last thing") stay spelled out.

  "Small and daily beats big and rare").

- **`traits`**: the 4-trait cap is gone; every trait is selectable.
- **`achieve`**: every option is a first-person answer to "A year in, what
  should have changed?": "I'm the person I imagined to be" · "I am finally
  disciplined" · "I trust myself more" · "My thinking got clearer" · "I'm

  interaction as before, restyled away from the reference app's look. The

  left). Both cards' rows are 52 pt so the whole screen, CTA included, fits
  an iPhone 14/15/16 Pro without scrolling. The "Between {start} and {end} ·
  your future self won't wake you" sentence is removed. (An earlier
  big-tile + 24-hour-band design was tried and rejected by the owner as too

  run-up) redesigned: one serif line ("We listened, {name}. Here's the
  plan."), then a swipeable, snapping carousel of cards with about three

  skips the entrances. Skipped steps still add nothing.

  question in both families, advancing one segment per answer; hidden on the
