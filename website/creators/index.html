What Future Self is

Future Self delivers daily quotes, affirmations and gentle reminders that keep people moving toward the person they want to become. It is a paid membership on iPhone, made by a small studio in the Netherlands. It is motivation, not therapy, not medical advice, and not a crisis service.

People you refer should know it is a membership (billed by the App Store) before they download. The current plans are weekly and yearly; the yearly plan can include a short free trial. Details live on the Terms of Service.

How it works

  1. Apply. Tell us who you are and how you would talk about the app. We read every application; acceptance is not automatic.
  2. We confirm the terms with you. If it is a fit, we send your referral link. We'll confirm your rate and payment details before you start.
  3. You share in your own words. When someone uses your link and becomes a paying member, you earn on that membership under the terms we sent you — not on views, clicks, or impressions.

Who it's for

People whose audience already cares about showing up: discipline, growth, a calmer mind, becoming someone they respect. You do not need a huge following. You do need to be honest about the product.

You must be 16 or older. The app is on iPhone first. It is not currently offered in the United Kingdom, so UK referrals cannot convert until that changes.

What you earn — and what you don't

You earn a share of paid memberships that start through your referral. We'll confirm your rate and payment details before you start. Rates can differ between people; a number on a landing page that we cannot honour is worse than no number.

This program is not:

  • pay-per-view, fan subscriptions, or selling content
  • ads, CPM, or a rate per thousand views (including anything like $0.50/1k)
  • payment for posting, likes, or reach
  • employment, or a promise of income — some accepted creators will earn little or nothing, especially before tracking is live

How to talk about it

  • Use your own words. Do not invent medical claims, guaranteed results, or a commission rate we have not confirmed with you.
  • Don't present Future Self as therapy, a medical device, or a crisis service.
  • Once you have a referral link, say so where advertising rules require it (for example that you may earn if someone joins).
  • Don't fake reviews, buy installs, or send spam. We will end a partnership over that.

Apply

Applications are reviewed by hand. If the form does not send, email the same details to hello@joinfutureself.com with the subject "Creator Program".

We'll use this only to review your application and reply. See the Privacy Policy. Until referral tracking is live, sending this does not start a paid partnership.

FAQ

Can I start earning today?

No. Tracking and payouts are not live. Applying now means we can review you first. You cannot earn from referrals until we accept you and tracking is on. We'll confirm your rate and payment details before you start.

What is the commission rate?

We'll confirm your rate and payment details before you start. We are not putting a percentage on this page.

When and how do I get paid?

Only after the conditions above. The payment method and schedule will be in the confirmation we send you — not before. We will not ask you to share a password or a one-time sign-in code.

Do I get a unique link when I apply?

Not automatically. Links come after we accept you and tracking is ready. You can talk about the app like anyone else in the meantime; those mentions will not be tracked.

Is this pay-per-view or CPM?

No. There is no PPV, no CPM, and no payment per thousand views. Earnings, when they exist, come only from paid memberships that start through your referral.

Do I need a huge audience?

No. Fit and honesty matter more than a follower count. We may still say no — the program is small on purpose.

Is the app free?

No. It is a paid App Store membership. A free trial may apply to the yearly plan. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; that is in the support pages.

Who can apply?

Anyone 16 or older with a genuine audience. Brands running unrelated ads, fake-engagement farms, and anyone asking us to invent a rate in public are not a fit.
