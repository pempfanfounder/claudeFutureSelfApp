FAQ

Do I have to show my face?

No. Face, voiceover, text, or a still of the notification are all fine. Use what already works for you.

Do I have to copy a specific style?

No. Keep your style. The example above is one way to show two Future Self moments, not a look we require.

How are referrals counted?

Someone uses your unique link or code and becomes a paying member. Views, likes and follows are not referrals. Tracking is not live yet; we will confirm how your link or code works before you start.

Do you pay for views?

Not now. You earn when people subscribe through your link or code. A view-pay plan may come later. We'll confirm your rate and payment details before you start.
