Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?

If your membership is not showing — after a new phone, a reinstall or switching devices — open the app and go to Settings → Account → Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the same App Store or Google Play account you originally purchased with; the store re-attaches your purchase within a few seconds.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed and managed by the app store, not by us:

  • iPhone / iPad: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Future Self, or visit apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Android: Play Store → your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions, or visit play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions.

Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple and Google under their store policies. On iOS, request one at reportaproblem.apple.com; on Android, use your Google Play order history. If you get stuck, email us and we will point you in the right direction.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, personalization answers, streaks, saved content and device records from our systems — it cannot be undone. Purchase records remain with Apple, Google and RevenueCat for billing purposes; the Privacy Policy has the details.

Can I change or pause my reminders?

Yes — open the app's settings to adjust when and how often reminders arrive, or to pause them. You can also manage notification permissions in your device's system settings at any time.
