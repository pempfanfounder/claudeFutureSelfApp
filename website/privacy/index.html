Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data Future Self collects, why, who processes it, and the rights you have. It is written to be readable — no legalese where plain words will do.

  • No ads, no selling of data, no tracking across other apps.
  • Our database, analytics and crash reporting run in the EU; subscription management (RevenueCat) runs in the US under EU Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • You can delete everything yourself, in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account.

2. What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

Account data
An anonymous account identifier, created automatically on first launch. If you choose to link a sign-in method, also your Apple or Google account identifier or your email address. You can use the app without ever providing an email.
Personalization answers
The choices you make during onboarding — such as the goals, obstacles and traits you select — and any free text you write, such as a life goal or a personal line.
Usage data
Pseudonymous product events (screens viewed, content ids, streak activity, experiment variant) tied to a random identifier rather than your name or email.
Purchase state
Your subscription status (for example: trial, active, expired, lifetime), passed to us from the app stores via RevenueCat. We never receive your card number or other payment details.
Device and notification data
Push token, platform, timezone, language and your notification preferences — used to deliver your reminders at the right local time.
Crash data
Technical error reports (such as device model, operating-system version and the state of the app when it crashed), so we can fix bugs.

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers.

3. What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent personal data — to anyone, ever.
  • We do not show ads and do not share your data with advertising networks.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • Your free-text answers and personal goals are stored only in our database and are never sent to analytics or crash-reporting services.

4. Why we process data (purposes and legal bases)

Under the GDPR, every use of personal data needs a legal basis. Ours are:

PurposeData usedLegal basis
Provide the service: your account, content personalisation, streaks, reminders and purchases Account data, personalization answers, device and notification data, purchase state Performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR)
Improve the product: pseudonymous analytics and A/B tests (for example, of onboarding copy) Usage data Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR); consent where your local law requires it
Keep the app working and safe: crash reporting and abuse prevention Crash data, account data Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR)

Where we rely on legitimate interests, we have balanced them against your rights and freedoms, and you can object at any time — see Your rights.

5. Processors and international transfers

We share personal data only with service providers (processors) that help us run Future Self, each bound by a data-processing agreement:

  • Supabase — database and authentication; hosted in the EU.
  • PostHog — product analytics; EU region.
  • Sentry — crash reporting; EU region.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management; located in the United States and receives only your account identifier and purchase state. This transfer is protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
  • Apple and Google push services — deliver notifications to your device.

We do not share personal data with any other third parties, unless the law requires it.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. Deleting your account — Settings → Account → Delete account — permanently removes your account, personalization answers, streaks, saved content and device records from our systems. Deletion cannot be undone.

Purchase records remain with Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required for billing and their own legal obligations; those records are governed by their policies.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live — and always where the GDPR applies — you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you, and receive a copy;
  • rectify data that is inaccurate;
  • erase your data ("right to be forgotten");
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • portability — receive data you gave us in a machine-readable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent.

The fastest way to erase everything is in the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. For anything else, email denizsahinbusiness@gmail.com; we respond within one month, as the GDPR requires.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data-protection authority — in the Netherlands, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens — or with the authority in the country where you live.

8. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (TLS) between the app and our servers, with access controls that limit who and what can reach the database, and by minimising what we collect in the first place — the most personal thing you give us, your own words, never leaves our database for analytics or crash tooling. No online service can guarantee absolute security; if you suspect a problem involving your data, contact us immediately.

9. Children

Future Self is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age that applies where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email us and we will delete it.

10. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above. If a change is material, we will also point it out in the app before it takes effect.
